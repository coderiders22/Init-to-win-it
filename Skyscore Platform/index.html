Welcome to SkyScore

SkyScore is your data-driven platform for quantifying flight operational difficulty at United Airlines. Analyze flight-level, baggage, and passenger data to get proactive insights for smoother turnarounds, efficient resource allocation, and on-time departures.

Quick Start Guide:

  • Discover Insights: Click the "Discover Insights" button in the hero section below to access your full dashboard with all analytics.
  • Check Flight Insights: Use the "Check Flight Insights" button in the navigation menu – it does the same thing as "Discover Insights" and takes you straight to the dashboard for detailed flight difficulty scores and trends.
  • Download Data: Once in the dashboard, use the "Download CSV Data" button to get the full dataset (skyscore_test_inittowinit.csv) for your own analysis.
  • Explore More: Scroll down to learn about our features in the About and Analysis sections.

About Us

Revolutionizing Airline Operations

SkyScore is a data-driven platform that quantifies the operational difficulty of flights for United Airlines. By analyzing flight-level, baggage, and passenger data, we provide proactive insights to ensure smoother turnarounds, efficient resource allocation, and on-time departures.

To empower frontline teams with predictive tools that identify high-difficulty flights and streamline airport operations for enhanced customer experience.

  • Data-driven flight difficulty scoring
  • Proactive resource allocation
  • Reducing delays & operational risk
