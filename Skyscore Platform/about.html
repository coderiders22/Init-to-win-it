About Us

Revolutionizing Airline Operations

SkyScore is a data-driven platform that quantifies the operational difficulty of flights for United Airlines. By analyzing flight-level, baggage, and passenger data, we provide proactive insights to ensure smoother turnarounds, efficient resource allocation, and on-time departures.

To empower frontline teams with predictive tools that identify high-difficulty flights and streamline airport operations for enhanced customer experience.

  • Data-driven flight difficulty scoring
  • Proactive resource allocation
  • Reducing delays & operational risk
  • Supporting smoother customer experience
